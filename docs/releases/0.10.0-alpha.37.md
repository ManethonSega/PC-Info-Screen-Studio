# Alpha.37: supplied Matrix sprites and object counts

The Matrix starfield now uses the supplied pixel artwork. Choose **White stars
1**, **Yellow stars 1** or **White stars 2**, then independently choose a white
shooting star, yellow shooting star or turn the shooting star off. Every choice
has native 4-, 5-, 6- and 10-dot variants.

**UFO** is a new animation built from the supplied gray UFO. Cat walk now uses
the five supplied bodies: Beige, Black, Gray, Orange and White. Its leg pixels
alternate while moving, and changing direction mirrors the full cat, including
its face and tail.

Rocket, crab, cat, UFO and meteor layers can show one, two or three objects.
Instances share the selected speed, direction, 60/90-dot area and play state,
and are spaced around the same movement cycle. Paused layers keep deterministic
positions.

The five supplied cat faces are also available as independent editable pixel-art
layers. The gallery now exposes 26 presets; eleven additional generated sprites
remain internal to animations.

Automated checks cover every supplied asset at all heights, cat mirroring and
colour choice, object spacing, star/shooting-star combinations, the UFO, actual
WPF selectors and settings persistence. Physical output and animation smoothness
still require owner qualification.
